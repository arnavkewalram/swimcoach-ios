SwimCoach — Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

SwimCoach does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data off your device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

What the app records

SwimCoach analyses swimming technique from video. Videos you record or import, the pose data extracted from them, and the resulting scores and detected faults are written only to the app's private storage on your iPhone.

Where that data goes

Nowhere. The app makes no network requests. Analysis runs entirely on the device using Apple's Vision framework and a Core ML model bundled inside the app. Nothing is uploaded, backed up to a server, or shared with the developer or any third party.

Permissions

  • Camera — to record a swim for analysis.
  • Microphone — audio is captured alongside the video so poolside coaching notes are audible on playback. Audio is never analysed and never leaves the device.

Importing an existing video uses the system photo picker, which runs outside the app and hands over only the item you choose. SwimCoach has no access to your photo library.

Sharing

You can export a video or a report card using the iOS share sheet. That is always something you initiate, and where it goes is entirely your choice — the app is not involved once the share sheet takes over.

Analytics and tracking

None. SwimCoach contains no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

SwimCoach is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

The About screen has a control that deletes every saved session and its video. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
